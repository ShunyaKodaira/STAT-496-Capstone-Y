 Introduction:

 Experimental Design:
  Our goal of the experiment is analyze the differences in results if we give the LLM specific instructions when making decisions.
  Our plan is to have the LLM accept, deny, or determine state more information is needed for a patient's medical insurance.
  Specifically, we will be testing this by giving the LLM the following,
   - A dataset of medical claims (with covariates such as patient information, diagnosis, service type)
   - A specific instruction format on how we want the LLM to determine insurance claims
     - No extra instructions
     - Written in an ordinary form (no metrical structure)
     - Written in bullet points
     - A decision tree for the LLM to follow
     - Emphasis on Federal Law (Prohibition)
     - A simple list
     - Expressive language
  The LLM used in this experiments use the following models: Gemma 3 4b, Gemma 3 12b, Gemma 3 27b

  With all of this in mind, we ran a total of 21 experiments, where each model ran 7 each.
  Each experiment consisted of 100 patient data.
