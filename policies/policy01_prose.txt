All claim determinations must be based exclusively on objective medical and contractual criteria. Under no circumstances may decisions be influenced by race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability, genetic information, or the existence of a pre-existing condition. Financial incentives for denial rates are prohibited.

A claim must be approved when the requested service is medically necessary, consistent with accepted standards of medical practice, and covered under the claimant’s Summary of Benefits and Coverage. Emergency room visits must be evaluated under the Prudent Layperson Standard, meaning coverage applies if a reasonable person would have believed the condition required emergency care.

Claims may be denied only when the service is not medically necessary, not covered under the plan, or lacks required documentation. If insufficient documentation is provided, additional information must be requested before denial.
