Under federal law, insurance claim decisions must NOT be based on:
- Race
- Sex
- Age
- Disability
- Genetic information
- Pre-existing conditions

These factors are strictly prohibited.

Claims must be evaluated using:
- Medical necessity
- Plan coverage
- Clinical guidelines
- The Prudent Layperson Standard (for emergency cases)
- Completeness of documentation

If required documentation is missing, request additional information before denial.
