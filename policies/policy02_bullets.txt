DO NOT CONSIDER:
- Race, color, national origin
- Sex or gender
- Age
- Disability
- Genetic information
- Pre-existing conditions
- Financial incentives
- Minor clerical errors in emergency cases

MUST CONSIDER:
- Is the treatment medically necessary?
- Does it meet accepted medical standards?
- Is the service covered under the plan?
- Does it meet clinical guideline criteria?
- Does the Prudent Layperson Standard apply (if emergency)?

OUTCOME RULES:
- Approve if medically necessary AND covered.
- Deny if not medically necessary OR not covered.
- Request more information if documentation is incomplete.
